Vector Free 0.3.0

Open-source bitmap to vector tracer. Engine in Rust, interface in Tauri.

Created by zavants (github.com/zavants/VectorFree), with Claude (Anthropic) as collaborator.

The tracing approach is inspired by published research on vectorizing anti-aliased images (J. Diebel, Bayesian Image Vectorization, Stanford, 2008). Vector Free is not affiliated with any other product.
